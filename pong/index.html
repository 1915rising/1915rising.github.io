PONG

The original arcade tennis game. First to 7 wins.

Left paddle: W / S  ·  Right paddle: ↑ / ↓ (two-player mode)  ·  1 vs Computer  ·  2 Two Players  ·  R Restart  ·  P / Esc Pause
Touch: drag on the court to move your paddle (two-player mode: left half = left paddle, right half = right paddle)
